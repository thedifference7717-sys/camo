[b]Fair launch: Dec 15, 2026, 18:00 UTC. Public stagenet open now.[/b]

[b]Hidden in plain sight.[/b] CAMO is digital cash that hides the sender, the receiver and the amount of every payment, with no opt-in and no exceptions. It runs Monero's audited privacy technology, unchanged.

[size=14pt][b]Why another coin?[/b][/size]
CAMO is a deliberately conservative Monero fork: the cryptography and consensus code are Monero's v0.18, kept as-is. What changes is parameters (name, ports, addresses, genesis, 1-minute blocks) and a launch done in the open: a public stagenet first, one disclosed allocation, and its own desktop wallet, Ghillie, ready on launch day.

[size=14pt][b]Mining[/b][/size]
Any modern CPU. Solo with your own node or Ghillie, solo with XMRig, or the official pool once it is online (1% fee to the public marketing wallet, PPLNS; link posted here). Mining needs free memory: close heavy apps if your hash rate drops to 0. Guide: [url]https://camocoin.org/mine.html[/url]

Build from source or use the test binaries, start a node, make a test wallet, mine, send: [url]https://camocoin.org/stagenet.html[/url] Seed nodes: 34.171.218.2:39740 and 3.134.132.214:39740 (both built into the software)

[size=14pt][b]Security[/b][/size]
[center][url=https://camocoin.org/assets/seals/seals-banner.png]The Seals, CAMO's watch team (image)[/url][/center]

Releases are reproducible and signed (two independent builders matched byte for byte). The Seals, CAMO's watch team, monitor the network around the clock for double-spend attempts, splits, deep reorgs, block bursts and hashrate swings, and track Monero's security fixes. On a possible attack they raise a public HOLD flag within minutes: [url]https://camocoin.org/status.html[/url], with a JSON feed exchanges and pools can poll to pause deposits automatically. The Seals watch and warn; nobody can pause or reverse the chain.

Release signing key: [tt]CAMO release signing <camocoin@proton.me>[/tt], fingerprint [tt]5F1A 8350 C748 BC43 AD19 2472 AF13 7B28 E11A 0857[/tt] ([url]https://camocoin.org/assets/camo-release.asc[/url], keys.openpgp.org). How to check a download: [url]https://camocoin.org/verify.html[/url]

Report problems privately: camocoin@proton.me or GitHub private vulnerability reporting.
