Network status

The Seals watch the CAMO network around the clock from its seed nodes. If they see a possible attack, this page turns to HOLD by itself within minutes, so exchanges and pools can pause deposits before anyone is cheated.

CHECKING…

Reading the Seal Watch feed.

    What the three states mean

    ALL CLEAR
    Nothing unusual. Normal confirmations are enough.
    CAUTION
    Something unusual, like a chain reorganization, a burst of blocks or a big hashrate swing. Often harmless, but services should wait for 30 or more confirmations. It clears by itself 30 minutes after the last sign.
    HOLD DEPOSITS
    A possible attack: the same coins spent twice, a chain split or a deep reorganization. Services should stop crediting CAMO deposits. It stays on for at least 2 hours, or until the team has checked the network. Coins already in your own wallet are not affected, and the chain itself keeps running: nobody can pause CAMO.

    For exchanges, pools and swap services

    Each seed publishes a small JSON file. Poll both every few minutes:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thedifference7717-sys/camo-status/main/seed1.json
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thedifference7717-sys/camo-status/main/seed2.json

    Fields: status (OK, CAUTION or HOLD), updated (unix time), until, reasons, height, network. Suggested rule: if either file says HOLD, pause crediting deposits; if either says CAUTION, or neither has updated in 45 minutes, require extra confirmations. Run your own CAMO node as well: the feed is an early warning, not a replacement for your own checks.

    The feed holds only public chain data (heights, transaction IDs). It never identifies anyone. The seeds can write to the status repository and nowhere else.
