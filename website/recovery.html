// YOUR 25 WORDS · ON PAPER ONLY

Recovery sheet

Your 25 recovery words are the only way back into your wallet if you lose your phone or computer. Print a blank sheet, write the words by hand, and keep it somewhere safe.

US LETTER (PDF)   A4 (PDF)

01 Rules that keep your coins safe

  • Write the words with a pen. Never type them into a computer, phone note, email, chat, cloud or photo.
  • Print the sheet blank. Never print a file that contains your words: printers and PDF readers can keep copies.
  • Make a second copy and keep it in another place (fire, flood, moving house).
  • Anyone with the 25 words has your coins. Nobody from CAMO will ever ask for them, for "support" or anything else.
